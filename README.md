Game Mechanics and Rules:
  - you start with 150 coins and 10 lives
  - your goal is to protect the end of the gray path (you) from the enemies that travel along the grey path
  - you can do that by buying towers that shoot projectiles at the enemies (a tower costs 50 coins) and placing them on the grass surrounding the path
  - there are 10 total waves of enemies, and each level gets progressively harder
  - you can also buy tower upgrades that cost more (there are three total tower levels)
  - every time you kill an enemy, you earn 15 coins
  - there are three types of enemies: regular, super fast, and super strong
  - you win by clearing all 10 waves and surviving
  - PRIZE FOR WINNING: nothing but you get bragging rights :D
